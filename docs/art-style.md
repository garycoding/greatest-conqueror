# Art style

The rules every picture in Greatest Conqueror follows. They were ruled on 2026-11-18 from [`art/style-sheet-1.png`](art/style-sheet-1.png) and [`art/worker-animation.gif`](art/worker-animation.gif). How sprites reach the screen (keys, anchors, team colour, the projection) is in [`architecture.md`](architecture.md#sprites).

## The style

Cartoon Modern: the mid-century graphic style of [`art/cartoon-modern-scene-1.png`](art/cartoon-modern-scene-1.png) and [`art/cartoon-modern-people-2.png`](art/cartoon-modern-people-2.png).

- Flat fields of muted colour, with a sponged grain over the whole picture.
- A thin dark line drawn loosely over the colour, which sits slightly out of register with it.
- Figures built from simple geometry: a large head, a simple body, thin limbs.
- Pattern used as decoration on cloth and walls.
- A small palette for each picture, from the palette of its age.
- Faces have an eye with its white and pupil, an ear, a touch of colour on the cheek, a mouth, and something of their own (a brow, a moustache, a scar).

## Noses

Small. Five forms are used, varied from one kind of person to the next, so that faces differ:

| Form | Description |
|---|---|
| A | A small curve |
| B | A button |
| C | A small hook |
| E | A line only |
| F | A bulb |

Not used: the upturned nose (D), and any large triangular nose.

## Palettes

One palette for each age: the ground, two accents, a light, and the line. The line is the same dark brown (`#2a2420`) in every age, and the teams' colours (`TeamColour`) are fixed across ages. An age's accents keep clear of every team's colour, so that nothing reads as team colour; the Industrial age's second accent was a navy (`#3f5f7a`) until it was found to match Shang's colour, and is bottle green (ruled 2026-11-19).

## Movement

The game moves at 12 frames a second; it does not use limited animation. (A first preview with two frames to a pose was rejected.)

- Figures are generated by code from jointed limbs (hip, knee and foot; shoulder, elbow and hand), not drawn frame by frame, so that every kind of person moves the same way and the frame count costs nothing to draw.
- Each pose is a cycle of 12 frames: standing (a breath, and now and then a blink), walking (a full stride: the knee bends as the leg swings forward, the foot plants and pushes off, the arms swing opposite the legs, and the body rises and falls), working (for example, a two-handed overhead chop with chips flying at the strike), and fighting.
- A figure is drawn in five views (ruled 2026-11-21): from the side facing right, from the front a quarter turn toward the right, from the front, from behind, and from behind a quarter turn toward the right; the drawing mirrors them for the facings toward the left. The figure kit (`FigureViews.swift`) turns the skeleton and the garments for each view; each kind draws its own hair, features and tools per view. The approved examples are the Stone-age worker and the car.
- [`art/worker-animation.gif`](art/worker-animation.gif) is the approved example: a Stone-age worker standing, walking and chopping.

## How the artwork is approved

The artwork is drawn in batches. Each batch is rendered, shown moving, and approved before it is merged.
